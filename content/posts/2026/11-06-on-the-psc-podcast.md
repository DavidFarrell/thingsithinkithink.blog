I had the pleasure recently to be on The PSC in Conversation with [Antonio Weiss](https://www.bennettschool.cam.ac.uk/about-us/person/antonio-weiss/), a Senior Partner at [The PSC](https://thepsc.co.uk) and former local councillor. As announced on the podcast, I've recently started working with The PSC as a senior AI expert advisor.

It was great to talk AI with Antonio, whose recent *AI Demystified* book was published by the FT and was an Amazon bestseller. I look forward to the second edition that comes out soon.

I loved the range and breadth of the conversation and the questions, and anybody who's interested can [access the podcast here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fUciQlYjYog).

As a preview, we discuss topics ranging from:

- The relationship between game design and AI system design
- Why public sector organisations undergoing AI transformation are missing a trick if they only focus on cost reduction
- Why the younger me would have pressed a button that says "do it for me", and why that button would have ruined me
- Why I use more AI than pretty much anybody and have no interest in AI music or AI books
